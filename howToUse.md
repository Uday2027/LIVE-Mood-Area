# How To Use MoodMap

Follow these steps to experience the city like never before!

### For New Users

#### 1. Checking the Vibes
When you instantly visit the homepage (`/`), you'll see a live view of the city map. You can pan, zoom, and check out what the mood in various parts of town looks like. No login required!

- Click on a **Neighborhood Overlay** to slide open the live activity panel.
- Inside the panel, you'll see up-to-the-minute **Mood Stories** and recent pins dropped by users inside that boundary.
- **Trends Page (`/trends`)**: Curious about the whole city? Head to the Trends link to view dynamic data charts matching neighborhood vibes over the last 24 hours.

#### 2. Joining the Map
Want to contribute to the map?
- Go to the **Map Page (`/`)** and simply click on your location or hit the "Pin" button.
- Choose a Mood.
- **Snap a picture!** The app will open your native device camera. You cannot upload a past photo; it must be the real mood, right now.
- Add a short description and place it.

#### 3. Creating an Account
- Head to the **Waitlist** or directly to **Sign Up (`/register`)**.
- Use our secure, client-validated Zod form to submit a password.
- By logging in, your dropped pins contribute to your overall **Credibility Score** and unlock Badges.
- **View your Profile (`/profile`)** to see your 90-day heatmap and generate your **MoodWrapped** shareable card!

### Vibe Circles (Live Real-Time Socializing)

- Ensure you have dropped a pin recently.
- If you and another user drop the same mood nearby, you'll see a **VibeMatch Popup**.
- Accept the match to be instantly dropped into a **Live Chat Room (Circle)**. 
- Talk quickly — the circle won't last forever!
